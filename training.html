How to Get the Most from Your Learning

  1. Choose a learning path. Start with a subject that interests you.
  2. Practise what you learn. Apply new knowledge through suitable exercises and small projects.
  3. Build evidence of your skills. Keep examples of your work and record the skills you develop in your YouthConnect profile.
  4. Check course details. Confirm whether resources are free, whether certificates cost money, and whether assessments are available.
  5. Understand professional requirements. Introductory learning resources do not replace recognized qualifications, supervised training, or professional registration where required.
  6. Research opportunities carefully. Check employer requirements, eligibility, and work authorization before applying abroad.
